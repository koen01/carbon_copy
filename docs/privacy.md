# Privacy Policy — Carbon Copy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Carbon Copy ("the app") is developed by Koen Erens as
free, open-source software. This policy explains what data
the app does and does not handle.

## Data collection

The app does not collect, transmit, store, or share any
personal data with the developer or any third party.

## Network connections

The app connects only to the Elegoo Centauri Carbon 2 printer
that you configure yourself, over MQTT and HTTP (MJPEG camera
stream) on your local network. This connection is made directly
from your device to your printer. No traffic is routed through
servers operated by the developer.

## Local storage

The app stores your configured printer host, LAN access code,
and serial number on your device using Android's standard
preferences storage, so it can reconnect to your printer. This
data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall
the app.

## Permissions

The app requests INTERNET permission to connect to your printer
over your local network. No other sensitive permissions are used.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not
knowingly process any data from children.
